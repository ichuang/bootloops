# Verifying a logical T on HGP data

The data and resource blocks use the same two HGP seed matrices. Physical masks X and Z specify an anticommuting pair of logical representatives in one block. The ideal-resource contract supplies an encoded T|+> in that mode of the resource block, factorized from other systems. The data and all spectators may be entangled with references.

## Logical instrument

For input basis bit b and joint Z_D Z_A result p, the surviving magic-state bit is p XOR b. Its phase exponent in powers of exp(iπ/4) is p XOR b. X_A readout x adds four times x(p XOR b). All four branches have probability 1/4. The frame S^p Z^x adds (2p+4x)b. The resulting relative exponent between b=1 and b=0 is one in every branch. Linearity gives T on an arbitrary input, including entangled inputs. An encoded Z on the resource adds four to that relative exponent.

For signed follow-on X readout the physical observable is F† X F, where F is the reported frame. For p=0 it is (-1)^x X; for p=1 it is (-1)^(x+1) Y. Physical Y strings also carry the known sign for i X_L Z_L. The checker evaluates each matrix entry of the corrected postmeasurement instrument on both input basis columns. It checks all raw results, record flips and residual logical Pauli classes. The relevant Boolean predicates are derived from that check. They test branch changes from wrong S corrections and account for Pauli errors that cancel a wrong readout bit. A Pauli along the final measurement axis is harmless on the projected output. Every surviving spectator Pauli generator is checked on both blocks.

## Physical schedule and fault contract

One GHZ meter qubit couples to each site of a chosen physical representative. A CNOT chain prepares the cat. Serial adjacent ZZ comparisons using fresh reset meters verify it. A flag of one rejects the run. Each cat qubit then controls its site's Pauli and is measured in X. The record parity reports the Pauli measurement. The compiler expands controlled Z and Y into elementary gates. The joint measurement and the resource X measurement each run for the requested number of rounds, requiring agreeing parities. A nondemolition logical X measurement followed by discarding the measured resource logical implements destructive logical readout while retaining its spectators. Physical arms for the subsequent data readout are compiled separately. Their signed outcomes consume the frame. Classical parity, branching and frame storage are exact. Both blocks have perfect input and terminal syndrome boundaries. Each listed preparation or readout admits an eigenvalue/record flip; each other elementary location admits any nonidentity Pauli on its quantum operands. Cat resets, checks and all couplings are included. The source-inclusive schedule additionally admits a Z error on the physical T preparation. Wall-clock storage, routing, classical hardware faults and loss are outside these schedules.

Forward propagation packs elementary fault experiments into bit planes. Reverse propagation independently transports output tests. SAT searches rule out a silent damaging assignment with distinct faulty locations, and the ftqc_codes LRAT checker replays the resulting proof against the reverse encoding. Threshold-three enumeration can instead group equal detector syndromes: two different locations form a silent damaging pair exactly when their syndrome matches and their logical signatures differ. The verifier repeats the grouping on reverse signatures.

## Structural lower bound for larger schedules

The checker parses every emitted gate against the verified-cat template. It verifies the ideal measured operator and the identity action on its commutant, equivalence of all round representatives modulo the original stabilizers, adjacency checks spanning the whole cat, repeated-read detectors, and the HGP distance d. The basis checks include signs. The following counting argument applies to the declared postselected Pauli-location model.

1. **Cat reset during verification.** With at most V faulty locations in a meter with V complete verification sweeps, either an entire sweep is clean or all V faults occur in the sweeps. In the first case, acceptance of the clean adjacent ZZ tests forces all cat bit errors to agree. A uniform cat X acts as a scalar on either GHZ phase. The residual cat phase errors can flip a readout. In the second case the cat preparation was clean. In either case, start tracking cat bit errors after the clean sweep or after ideal preparation, respectively.
2. **Spread.** During verification cat qubits are controls, and the shared target is retired after each pair comparison. A single verification fault can create a bit flip on at most one cat qubit. Cat phase errors never spread to data. After verification each cat qubit controls one data operand only. Thus t faults add an effective data Pauli on at most t sites, with each contributing fault associated with at most one site in its round's support. A pre-existing data Pauli passes through to the same data site and can flip a meter record. Cat phase effects or faults solely on measurement records can affect their own round. These statements hold separately for each accepted transcript.
3. **Repeated measurements.** Let R be the number of equivalent representatives in a stage and L the maximum number containing any one physical site. A fault in a previous stage can affect at most L readout rounds via its single residual data site. A fault within this stage can affect its own round plus subsequent visits to that site, again at most L rounds in total, since its own round contains the site. A fault with no residual data site can affect at most its own round. With tL < R at least one round reads the correct ideal value. Agreement of all the records then fixes the reported value. Apply this in time order, including the chosen Clifford-frame arm.
4. **Final data.** If t < d and the terminal stabilizer syndrome vanishes, an effective data Pauli of weight at most t is a stabilizer on both blocks. It preserves every encoded mode and the decoded logical channel. Combining the three bounds excludes every damaging accepted pattern with weight below min(d, V+1, min_stages ceil(R/L)). For an upper witness the checker flips one individual cat readout per joint-parity round, and independently propagates those faults through the actual schedule.

This is a structural mathematical certificate whose counting lemma is part of the trusted argument. It does not enumerate all weight-seven faults or provide a Lean derivation. In the 450-qubit study the joint-Z and resource-X stages have disjoint representatives within each stage. The physical X arm also has disjoint supports. The physical Y arm has maximum incidence two.

## Preparing a resource

The in-situ benchmark implements the single-site product-state projection in Liu et al., arXiv:2604.05126, Sec. III. At the unique overlap of X_L and Z_L prepare a raw T state. Other X_L sites start in |+>, and the other sites in |0>. Sequential Clifford meters measure every CSS check. Accept only all positive results. This schedule uses the paper's single-site injection principle with a separately specified acceptance policy and gate ordering.

For the product embedding V, X_L V = V X and Z_L V = V Z. The code projector Π commutes with both representatives. Consequently V†ΠV is scalar and the accepted embedding transmits the target qubit faithfully. Its success probability is obtained on raw |0> by counting independent random CSS syndromes. A Z just after the raw preparation is identical to Z_L on the initialized subspace and survives accepted projection. The branch checker then obtains Z_L T_L on data. This proves an accepted weight-one counterexample under the source-inclusive model independently of any stochastic error-rate estimate.
