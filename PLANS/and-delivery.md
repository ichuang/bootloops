# Accepted three-block AND delivery

The target is one fresh ordered resource (u,v,t) whose logical state is H on the third wire of CCZ applied to |+++>. The encoding is Golay [[23,1,7]] in each of 47 outer QR coordinates. Give the canonical positive frame, or record and discharge its correction. The consuming AND branch also tracks X on the target when both measurement bits are one.

At physical p = 1/10000 the study asks for the following handoff.

* Bind the accept bit to the same attempt, ordered resource and ready tick. Give a strictly positive lower bound on its probability. Deliver once, with rejected attempts discarded. Use disjoint locations and fresh randomness across attempts and the online computation. Bounds must hold under the allowed history-dependent Pauli labels, including after conditioning. Disjoint hardware by itself proves independence of the fault indicators, not of adversarial label choices.
* Conditional on acceptance, the probability charged for unusable outputs is at most 10^-15 per triple. The current consumer adds two sector charges of 5×10^-16 each. Prove a charge allocation (or a single shared junk event charged once by an equivalent ledger). Splitting a general upper bound in half does not establish two sector claims.
* Apart from junk, identify Pauli supports, or spans of operators with those supports, relative to the ideal entangled resource. For each increasing event of dirty columns, including union with independent incoming dirt, its joint probability with being good, conditional on acceptance, is bounded by its probability under 47 independent Bernoulli indicators of rate 10^-6 (unit mode weight). One sufficient construction dominates the union of the residual column supports of all three blocks and both sectors by a common such mask. This permits arbitrary within-column correlations. If using separate sector bounds, prove the union bounds used when a CZ mixes sectors.
* The 141 inner blocks arrive at the post-inner-correction boundary. The consumer assigns three X location classes and X atoms from plus and zero, and two Z classes and a zero Z atom, per output block within a column. Prove these residuals and their joint use with factory dirt, or prove an enclosing bound for the combined cones. A syndrome test or an ideal decode alone cannot establish this physical boundary.
* Record output frame, code matrices, schedule, source locations and acceptance evidence. Expected physical locations per accepted triple include retries, consumed ancillas and idles up to the ready tick. Separate a source's wire-tick estimate from the manuscript's physical ledger.

## Controls

Hadamard on the third wire gives amplitude [t=uv]/2. A Hadamard on the first wire fails this test. One shared fault of probability 10^-6 on six specified columns passes all marginal tests, but their joint probability exceeds 10^-36 plus even the full junk allowance. Conditioning must use the acceptance denominator of the actual attempt. A one-sided junk rate of 10^-15 exceeds one sector's current charge.

The executable consumer checks and physical experiment belong with the masking study in the research repository, under `ftqc_masking_v3a_fable/and_delivery`. Existing entry points are `ftqc_uncompute.blocks.gadget_identities`, `ftqc_gadgets.magic_transfer` and `ftqc_magic.delivery.read_delivery`. This study introduces no new verifier.

## Physical probe at p = 1/10000

With deterministic fresh CSS transfer before distillation, every raw-control fanout permits the same syndrome-free logical Z. A minimum QR-47 representative gives 11 such sites. Independent faults on them generate hidden parity with probability 0.00109890066. Postselected RM15 followed by the eight-input cube gives conditional CCZ error 6.08016×10^-14 even with ideal subsequent processing. The full junk allocation would allow an input rate of only 0.000554468098 in this design. An explicit six-location pattern passes both distillation checks. The research study replays the physical witnesses, exact accepted-mask laws and the full-source-noise lower-bound argument for a fixed admissible Pauli labeling. Extending the composition verifier would leave this injection obstruction in place.
